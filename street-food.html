Street Food Tips

🕒 Best Time to Go

Most stalls come alive after 5 PM, with kottu stands staying open past midnight.

💵 Cash is King

Carry small notes — most street vendors don't accept cards or digital payments.

🌶️ Spice Level

Jaffna food runs spicy by default — ask for "less chilli" if you prefer it mild.
